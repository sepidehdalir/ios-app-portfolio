# Zakat Clear: Nisab Calculator

**A clear record of a monetary Zakat estimate.**

Enter selected assets and deductions, document a manual metal price or threshold, review hawl status and save assessment snapshots. An estimation aid, not a religious ruling.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zakat-clear-nisab-calculator/id6814923109)
