# MirrorLux: Makeup Mirror

**A pocket mirror for everyday touch-ups.**

A live front-camera mirror with zoom, mirrored views and screen-edge lighting. Optional Pro adds additional controls, freeze frame and deliberate snapshot saving.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mirrorlux-makeup-mirror/id6797422767) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/apps/mirrorlux/)

## Product and engineering

Camera lifecycle; entitlement-gated controls; add-only Photos permission; clear distinction between a transient preview and an explicitly saved snapshot.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, AVFoundation, StoreKit 2, Photos.
