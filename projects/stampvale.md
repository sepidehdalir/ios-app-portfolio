# Stampvale: Photo Date Stamp

**Deliberate dates and captions for existing job photos.**

Choose a capture date, manual calendar date or no date; add a caption and optional typed place; preview a readable label and export a separate PNG copy. Originals stay unchanged. Single-photo export and every label style are free. A one-time Lifetime Pro purchase adds batches up to 40 photos and up to 12 reusable caption/style presets.

## Product and engineering

Native Universal iPhone/iPad utility targeting iOS 15+. Strict Gregorian capture metadata interpretation preserves the written calendar day; missing or invalid dates require an explicit choice. ImageIO/CoreGraphics/CoreText rendering normalizes all 8 EXIF orientations and exports 8-bit sRGB PNGs without copied source metadata, including GPS. Sequential bounded rendering, partial/cancelled results, native sharing and add-only Photos save protect the workflow. StoreKit 2 verifies entitlements and supports restoration.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, PHPicker, ImageIO, Core Graphics, Core Text, StoreKit 2. No account, ads, AI service, tracking or product backend. Production source is private.

Dated local evidence covers deterministic date/rendering/source-retention tests, native provider/export/preset/StoreKit cases, real picker and save/share workflows, phone/tablet Pro flows, large-type accessibility and dark landscape use. Physical execution, minimum iOS 15 runtime and Apple-server paid billing remain unverified. Dates are editable labels, not trusted evidence. Campaigns use authentic final native UI and real app-rendered copies/previews with owned illustrative demonstration photos.
