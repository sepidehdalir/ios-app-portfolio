# Tendarchive: Home Maintenance

Keep the work. Know what’s next.

A private native iPhone and iPad maintenance record: choose your own schedule, record actual completed work and retrieve its date, cost, provider, part, receipt reference and next due date later.

## A service record that answers the next question

Owners create a home and an editable starter or custom task. Dates follow their instructions, with explicit recurrence choices. A completed service preserves the actual date, recorded cost and currency, provider and part snapshots, notes and receipt reference. Corrections, Undo and recoverable Trash retain the record. No missed service event is invented.

Pro adds new owned photos and original PDF attachments. Every selected source is reviewed before it is used; retained originals remain readable and exportable if Pro ends. Free includes a complete basic maintenance PDF and full attachment backup/recovery. Imports create fresh archived copies while preserving current work. Advanced reports include chosen photo pages, CSV and per-currency costs without exchange conversion.
