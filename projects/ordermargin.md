## Product workflow

For a solo maker, baker, florist or custom-order business: enter material consumption, labor minutes and hourly rate, packaging, delivery and other costs. Add overhead and compare markup, target estimated margin or a custom selling price. Customer tax stays separate from internal profit. Each order retains its currency and rate snapshots; no exchange-rate conversion or mixed-currency totals.

Create a customer PDF with scope, client, quote number, total, deposit, manual payments, balance and terms. Internal costs, rates and estimated profit stay private. Orders progress through explicit statuses; optional local due reminders, recoverable Trash, archives and portable backups support the ongoing work. Manual payment records do not move money.

Free includes three active orders, calculations, manual payments, Classic PDF, material library and backups. Optional annual or one-time Lifetime Pro adds unlimited active orders, order/labor templates, business branding, advanced monthly reports, CSV export and Atelier PDF styling, subject to safe local-storage limits. Apple displays the actual localized price; Lifetime does not cancel an existing annual subscription.

## Engineering

Foundation Decimal pricing, compatible material-unit conversions, currency-aware rounding, versioned validated Codable backups and atomic repository writes. Imports preserve existing work and add fresh record identities/quote numbers; larger Free backups can be imported as archived work. Native paginated PDFs and internal CSV export have separate privacy boundaries. StoreKit2 handles verified annual/Lifetime access, renewal/expiry/grace, cancellation, pending/error states and restore. A serial native reminder scheduler prevents overlapping foreground/save replacements from removing partially added requests.

No developer backend, analytics, advertising, tracking or account. Data stays local unless shared to a destination selected by the user; Apple purchases, device backups and selected sharing services follow their own policies. Commercial source, signing material and private evidence remain private. Profit figures are estimates from entered costs; the app does not perform bookkeeping, determine tax or process payments.
