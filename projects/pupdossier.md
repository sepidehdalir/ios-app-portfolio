Pupdossier keeps the history an owner already has: eight typed care-record categories, exact owner-entered dates and time zones, recorded kg/lb measurements, chosen photos and original PDFs. Completing a record never generates a next care schedule. Generic local reminders keep pet names and clinical details out of notification text.

A deliberate A4 or Letter PDF handoff starts with private notes, contacts and identifiers off. Owners preview and choose what to include. Original PDFs retain their supplied bytes and metadata; chosen images are normalized without private camera/location metadata. Recoverable Trash and full attachment backups/imports preserve current work, with fresh archived copies on import.

Free includes one active pet, core records, recorded weights, generic reminders, five retained attachment slots plus a profile photo, a basic full-record PDF and full recovery/backup/import. Optional Lifetime Pro adds more pets/slots, custom Vet visit and Caregiver handoff reports, chosen photo appendix, CSV and full past reminder history. No subscription; technical file/storage limits apply.

Native Universal iPhone/iPad, min15/currentSDK26.5, protected local storage. No developer account, backend, collection, tracking, analytics integration, AI or sync. Record keeping only: no diagnosis, recommended care dates, dose calculations, clinical weight interpretation, vaccine verification or boarding/travel eligibility decision.
