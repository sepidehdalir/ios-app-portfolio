# Her Daily Prayer Cards

**Daily prayer cards, favorites and gentle reminders.**

A Christian prayer-card app for reflection, with saved favorites, reminders and shareable cards. Premium expands the library, categories and share templates.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/her-daily-prayer-cards/id6788279130) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/her-daily-prayer-cards/)

## Product and engineering

Content organization; saved favorites; notification scheduling; share templates and a clear premium content boundary.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, StoreKit, UserNotifications.
