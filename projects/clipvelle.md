# Clipvelle: Video to Audio

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sepidehdalir/clipvelle/main/docs/assets/icon.png" width="112" alt="Clipvelle icon">

Keep a video's sound as a clearly named audio file. Clipvelle imports a local video, exposes individual audio tracks, previews and trims a contiguous segment, then streams M4A or WAV output with inspected duration and size.

**Status · October16,2026:** In development; not submitted or released. App Store Connect accepted the app name. Native core tests passed, including a30-minute extraction and independent batch failure handling. StoreKit local tests passed on iOS26.2 for purchase/restore/pending/cancel/network error/revocation. Final UI/device QA, marketing and release preparation continue. These tests do not establish production purchases or demand.

Swift/SwiftUI, AVFoundation, PhotosUI, StoreKit2 and XCTest. Universal iPhone/iPad, iOS15 deployment. Processing stays on-device; no account, media upload, ads or tracking SDK. Free single-video exports; Lifetime Pro adds batch and reusable presets.
