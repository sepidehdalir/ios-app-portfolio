# Clipvelle: Video to Audio

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sepidehdalir/clipvelle/main/docs/assets/icon.png" width="112" alt="Clipvelle icon">

Keep a video's sound as a clearly named audio file. Clipvelle imports a local video, exposes individual audio tracks, previews and trims a contiguous segment, then streams M4A or WAV output with inspected duration and size.

**Status · October 16, 2026:** V1 built; Apple-validated signed build 1.0 (1) uploaded, processed and selected. Native core/UI and local StoreKit QA passed on iOS 17.0/26.2, including a 30-minute extraction, batch per-file failures, purchase/restore/pending/cancel/error/refund and offline entitlement. Premium iPhone and iPad campaigns are saved to ASC. Physical iPhone 26.1 core media, full export/share/relaunch and remote product lookup also passed. Final trim/Files completion, sandbox transactions and owner privacy declaration remain open. **Not submitted or released.** Local tests do not establish production transactions or market demand.
