# Tileleaf: Poster Printing

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sepidehdalir/tileleaf-showcase/main/docs/assets/icon.png" alt="Tileleaf app icon" width="140">

Small sheets. Bigger ideas. Tileleaf expands a selected image or PDF page across numbered A4/Letter sheets, with physical artwork dimensions, an assembly map, a printed50mm scale check and stable selected-tile reprints.

**October19,2026:** In production. App Store Connect accepted the name; not submitted or publicly released.15native unit checks and the compact-phone free-export/Pro-gate workflow passed. Actual measured print output, iPad/device/live StoreKit and release checks remain open. TestFlight skipped by publisher.

Native SwiftUI/UIKit, Core Graphics/PDFKit, ImageIO, CryptoKit and StoreKit2. Universal iPhone/iPad deployment15+. Local projects retain app-owned sources and layout identity. Free complete export up to4artwork tiles; one-time LifetimePro for larger bounded grids, local projects and replacement exports. No subscription, account, content upload or analytics SDK.

Independent numeric/PDF inspections cover nondivisible edges and replacement-page identity. Local Xcode StoreKit proof is kept separate from genuine Sandbox/production purchase evidence. Printer scaling still requires actual measured paper proof.
