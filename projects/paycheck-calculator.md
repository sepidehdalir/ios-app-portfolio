# Paycheck Calculator: W-4

**Understand a take-home pay estimate.**

US paycheck planning with earnings inputs, W-4 choices, deductions and a visible calculation breakdown. Estimates are not a guarantee of payroll results.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paycheck-calculator-w-4/id6815119897) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/apps/paycheck-calculator/)

## Product and engineering

Pay-period and tax-base calculations; W-4 input handling; transparent assumptions and estimated withholding breakdowns.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, Foundation.
