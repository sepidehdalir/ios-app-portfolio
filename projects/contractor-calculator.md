# Contractor Calculator: Jobsite

**Material estimates with the math explained.**

Concrete, paint and square-up calculations, with project history, waste allowances and ordering increments. Optional Lifetime Pro adds decking, stairs and board feet.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/contractor-calculator-jobsite/id6815061123) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/apps/contractor-calculator/)

## Product and engineering

Dimensional input; material-specific rounding; transparent calculation steps; useful free tools with clearly labeled paid tools.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, Foundation, StoreKit 2.
