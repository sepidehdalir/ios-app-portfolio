# Discount Calculator · Dealora

**See the final price behind a sale.**

Sequential discounts, entered sales-tax rates, unit-price comparisons and a clear price breakdown.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/discount-calculator-dealora/id6815449806) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/apps/dealora/)

## Product and engineering

Sequential percentage calculations; comparison across package sizes using the same unit; explicit tax and savings breakdowns.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, Foundation.
