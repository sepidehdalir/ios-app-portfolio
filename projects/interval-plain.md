# Interval Plain: Routine Timer

**Your routine. Today’s time.** Fit a saved routine to available minutes and seconds while keeping chosen stages unchanged.

Set flexible-stage minimums, inspect every original/fitted duration, see unused seconds or an infeasible minimum, share actual timing text and recover the fitted sequence paused. The original routine remains intact. Time fitting and one reusable routine are free; optional Lifetime Pro adds a named library, duplication and ordering.

[Live product and premium campaigns](https://sepidehdalir.github.io/intervalplain-showcase/) · [Showcase repository](https://github.com/sepidehdalir/intervalplain-showcase)

## Product and engineering

Swift, SwiftUI, AVFoundation and StoreKit2; Universal iOS/iPadOS15+. Bounded proportional allocation with stable integer rounding, separate validated plans, atomic local persistence and checkpoint recovery. A dedicated two-column iPad workspace adapts to stacked phone/accessibility layouts. Foreground timing pauses when leaving/locking; no background-cue or deadline guarantee. No account, analytics or developer upload service.

October19 build4 QA: 25 core tests plus native model/local StoreKit checks, iPhone/iPad workflow/accessibility and small iPhone/iOS17 maximum text passed. Current physical-device, actual iOS15 and Apple-server purchase behavior remain unverified. Separate phone/tablet campaigns contain authentic final UI.
