# Interval Plain: Routine Timer

**Build a work/rest routine and follow spoken cues.**

One reusable routine is free. Optional Lifetime Pro adds a saved routine library, duplication and reordering. A foreground timer with honest pause behavior when interrupted.

[Dedicated showcase](https://github.com/sepidehdalir/intervalplain-showcase)

## Product and engineering

Continuous-clock timing; missed-stage detection; local speech; atomic persistence; entitlement restoration and interruption recovery.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, AVFoundation, StoreKit 2.
