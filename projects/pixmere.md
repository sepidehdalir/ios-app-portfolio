# Pixmere: Photo Compressor

**Prepare photos for file-size and pixel constraints.**

A focused image utility for compression, resizing and export workflows with clear output constraints.

[Dedicated showcase](https://github.com/sepidehdalir/filefit-showcase)

## Product and engineering

Image encoding; bounded export work; output verification; preserving a clear relationship between the requested constraints and exported result.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, ImageIO, PhotosUI, StoreKit 2.

## Release status

**October 19, 2026:** app Rejected under Guideline 4.3(a); Lifetime Pro Ready for Review. Apple confirmed receipt of the App Review Board appeal. Decision pending; no new binary resubmission, approval or release claimed.
