# Lumino Daily Reflection

**A quiet space for reflection and journaling.**

Daily reflection cards, journaling, a personal archive and calming soundscapes. Presented as reflection rather than a prediction or clinical service.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lumino-daily-reflection/id6786564391) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/apps/lumino/)

## Product and engineering

Local journal persistence; reflection history; audio and widget integration; product copy that states the actual experience.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, Core Data, AVFoundation, StoreKit, WidgetKit.
