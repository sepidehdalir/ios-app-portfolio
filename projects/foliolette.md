# Foliolette: Print to Size

**The photo sizes you choose, on one printable sheet.**

Import photos, choose physical dimensions, preview crops and arrange A4 or Letter sheets for PDF export and system printing.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/foliolette-print-to-size/id6816755162) · [Dedicated showcase](https://github.com/sepidehdalir/foliolette-showcase) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/apps/foliolette/)

## Product and engineering

Physical-dimension layout; crop versus fit decisions; mixed print sizes; PDF output and native printing.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, ImageIO, PDFKit, PhotosUI, StoreKit 2.
