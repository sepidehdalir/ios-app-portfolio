## From a package to a clear statement

Create a student and a package with its session grant, quoted charge, currency, cancellation policy and optional expiry. Schedule a lesson without consuming a session; record attendance deliberately. Corrections retain a reason and adjust credits once. Rescheduling reopens the same lesson and returns a prior deduction. Explicit rollover links both package histories while leaving historical payments with their original package.

Record money received and refunds manually. A payment does not consume a session, and attendance does not invent payment. Currency-specific balances and monthly cash summaries preserve the distinction. Preview A4 or US Letter statements and receipts; private student notes are excluded, and lesson notes stay off statements unless explicitly included. Manual records do not transfer money, verify bank settlement or create tax invoices.

Free includes three active students, basic lessons/packages, corrections, rollover, manual payments, basic PDFs and portable backup/import. Optional Annual or one-time Lifetime Pro adds unlimited active students, finite recurring series, teacher branding, historical cash summaries, payment CSV and calendar files, within local storage and safe file limits. Apple displays localized prices. Lifetime does not cancel an existing annual subscription.
