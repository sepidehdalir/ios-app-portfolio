## Product and visual direction

Return to a project already in progress. Choose one priority, keep a next small step, focus for10/20/custom minutes and review actual time and cumulative progress before saving. Custom crafts and units support sewing, models, pottery, painting and more. Materials are manually entered remaining balances. Positive timed progress supports clearly labeled estimates; a finished gallery and native cards preserve an outcome. This is a completion journal rather than a specialized row counter, pattern reader, marketplace or social service. Existing competitors informed the scope; no market-novelty or demand claim.

The original folded-ribbon identity uses ivory, indigo, saffron and lilac. Five distinct phone and five dedicated tablet compositions form value → workflow → outcome, followed by estimates and cross-craft units. Screens and completion outputs are authentic native captures; artwork and example records are original and fictional.

## Native engineering

SwiftUI/UIKit, Universal min iOS/iPadOS15 with SDK26.5, protected atomic local JSON, Apple photo picker/camera, ImageIO, CryptoKit, StoreKit2 and UserNotifications. No account, developer backend, tracking or analytics integration. Integer quantities, localized decimal input, stable linked identities and persisted focus drafts support reliable corrections and relaunch. Bounded hash-checked photo backups import fresh archived copies while preserving current work; unreadable originals and recoverable Trash protect recovery. Names/photos are opt-in for completion output; private notes stay out of cards/PDF/CSV. Generic reminders are optional.

## Monetization

Useful Free core with three active/paused projects and unlimited saved history. Safety backup/import, existing records, recovery and essential corrections stay Free. One optional Lifetime Pro purchase adds project/photo capacity, advanced estimates, insights, CSV and extra card styles. Prices are supplied by Apple; Restore Purchases is available. No subscription.
