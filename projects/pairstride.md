## Product

A complete free workflow includes two-clip manual alignment, linked playback,1×/½×/¼× speed, labels, one available source audio or mute and labeled720p side-by-side export. A separate checked movie can be reviewed, saved to Files or Photos, or shared. Original clips remain unchanged.

Optional non-consumable Lifetime Pro adds up to10 saved local projects with owned media, recoverable Trash, stacked portrait layout,1080p export and up to20 label/output presets. Active and trashed media share a2GB limit. No subscription, account, backend or tracking/analytics upload.

## Engineering

SwiftUI, AVFoundation, Core Image, Core Text, PhotosUI and StoreKit2. A single composition and custom renderer drive both preview and export. The overlap plan makes retained source ranges explicit; completed output is checked for dimensions, duration, audio and decodable frames before atomic publication. Durable projects own their source copies and verify SHA256 manifests on reopen.

Native tests independently decode paired event flashes, compare rotated source corners to exported pixels, measure selected-source audio frequencies and slow-speed pitch, cancel in-flight exports and check original hashes. Storage tests cover relaunch/recovery, corruption preservation and Trash. Small iPhone/iOS17, iPad/iOS17 and current iPhone/iOS26.5 workflows passed. Large type and Dark Mode were inspected; one exact-heading automated clipping false positive was manually documented. iOS15 compilation is verified; that runtime was unavailable. The publisher reported the physical real-video import/alignment/audio/export/save workflow passed on iOS26.1. The publisher also reported Apple-confirmed Sandbox purchase, saved-pair relaunch/reopen and restore passed. These reports are distinct from automated evidence; no production paid transaction is claimed.

Public materials are source-free; commercial code is privately backed up. Original illustrative motion and authentic final app UI underpin a dedicated graphite, ivory, cobalt and burnt-orange campaign. Supported inputs and manual-alignment/30fpsSDR limitations are explicit.
