# Culina: Cooking Converter

**Recipe conversions with ingredient context.**

Ingredient-aware volume/weight estimates, recipe scaling, kitchen fractions and temperature conversions with explicit measure conventions.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/culina-cooking-converter/id6815367326) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/apps/culina/)

## Product and engineering

Ingredient reference data; unit conventions; preparation/density notes; practical rounding and editable recipe rows.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, Foundation.
