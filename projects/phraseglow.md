# Phraseglow: Big Text Cards

**Your words, a little clearer.** A native Universal iPhone and iPad app for everyday face-to-face communication: type a message, show readable big text, turn it toward someone opposite and optionally speak it with an installed Apple voice.

**Status: app1.0(1) and Lifetime Pro submitted together on October18,2026; both Waiting for Review. V1 frozen; not released.**

The product keeps basic communication free: no usage limit on display/speech, all palettes and three saved phrases. A one-time Lifetime Pro purchase adds more phrases, named collections and saved styles. Existing content remains accessible after entitlement changes. No subscription, account, product backend, translation or medical treatment claims.

Engineering: SwiftUI with native UIKit text input and measured text fitting, natural bidirectional scripts, predictable edit/show/turn/return state, a readable long-text floor with scrolling, atomic versioned Codable storage and recovery errors, AVSpeechSynthesizer lifecycle/route handling, StoreKit2 verified entitlements and localized pricing. Universal iOS/iPadOS15+; purposeful wide tablet workspace and stacked narrow layouts.

Core, local StoreKit and iPhone/iPad automated tests passed. Physical speech completion/route and injected notification handlers passed; no subjective listening claim. Genuine remote catalog verified. Remaining remote Sandbox purchase/restore/entitlement/offline checks were explicitly waived by the publisher after a failed initial purchase request; skipped is never described as passed. iOS15 deployment compiles; the oldest available runtime used for execution was iOS17. TestFlight skipped by publisher instruction.
