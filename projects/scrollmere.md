# Scrollmere: Long Screenshot

**Turn overlapping screenshots into one long image.**

Import screenshots, inspect overlap suggestions, correct alignment and export a long image. Optional Pro includes longer sets and paginated PDF export.

[Dedicated showcase](https://github.com/sepidehdalir/scrollmere-showcase) · [Product site](https://arvinify.com/apps/scrollmere/)

## Product and engineering

Overlap estimation; manual correction and recovery; image/PDF export; on-device processing.

**Technology:** Swift, SwiftUI, ImageIO, PDFKit, StoreKit 2.

## Release status

**October 19, 2026:** app Rejected under Guideline 4.3(a); Lifetime Pro Ready for Review. Apple confirmed receipt of the App Review Board appeal. Decision pending; no new binary resubmission, approval or release claimed.
