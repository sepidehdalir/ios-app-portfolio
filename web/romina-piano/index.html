A few notes

Before we play.

Can I start if I’m a complete beginner?

Absolutely. Lessons can begin with the basics and grow at a pace that feels comfortable. You don't need prior experience.

What ages can take lessons?

Romina teaches children, teens, and adults. Send a short note about the student’s age and experience so she can recommend a good starting point.

Do online lessons work well?

Online lessons are available worldwide. A piano or keyboard, a stable internet connection, and a camera angle that shows your hands are helpful. Romina can help you check your setup.

How do fees and online payment work?

Lesson fees depend on the format and length. After you discuss your needs and a time with Romina, she’ll confirm the price and send a secure online payment link. No payment is taken through this preview site.

Which languages are lessons available in?

The site presents Romina’s lessons in Persian and English. Please message her to confirm which language best suits your lesson.
