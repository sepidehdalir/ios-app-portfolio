## Engineering approach

- Native Swift and SwiftUI interfaces, with Apple frameworks selected for each product.
- Clear constraints and recovery paths around image processing, printing, timers and calculators.
- StoreKit where a paid upgrade fits the product; useful free functionality and explicit paid boundaries.
- Test evidence and release status kept distinct: compilation, device execution, store submission and public availability each have their own checks.
- App-specific privacy descriptions and deliberate permission requests.

This is a curated public portfolio. Commercial production repositories are private under the publisher's source policy. Public case studies contain product descriptions, reviewed visuals and engineering summaries; they do not expose signing material, credentials or user data.
